# Sportza User Guide

This guide explains how end users can use Sportza for booking, matches, ratings, open play, and training.

## 1) Getting Started

### Create or access your account

1. Open Sportza web or app.
2. Select **Login**.
3. Choose one method:
   - Google Sign-In
   - Email OTP
   - Magic Link
4. Complete verification to enter your dashboard.

### Complete your profile

- Add your name, phone, and city.
- Choose your preferred sports.
- Review your role view (Player, Venue Owner, or Trainer).

## 2) Player Guide

### Book a venue

1. Go to **Venues**.
2. Filter by city, sport, date, and time slot.
3. Select venue and court.
4. Confirm slot availability.
5. Proceed to payment and complete booking.

### Join or host open play

1. Open **Open Play**.
2. Browse sessions by sport, skill level, and location.
3. Tap **Join** to participate.
4. To host, tap **Create Open Play**, set rules, capacity, and timing.

### Play competitive matches

- Create a match or accept an invite.
- Add teams and score updates.
- Complete match with final result.
- Ensure match type is `COMPETITIVE` or `TOURNAMENT` for rating impact.

### Check your Sportza rating

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Go to **Stats/Rating**.
3. Select sport and format (Overall, Singles, Doubles, etc.).
4. Review:
   - Current rating
   - Confidence tier
   - Matches played
   - Recent rating trend/history

Note: Only `COMPETITIVE` and `TOURNAMENT` matches affect rating.

## 3) Venue Owner Guide

### Manage venues and courts

- Add venue details, address, and amenities.
- Configure courts/turfs and surface type.
- Set operating hours and available slots.

### Manage bookings

- View incoming bookings in the owner dashboard.
- Confirm slot status and player details.
- Handle cancellations and refunds according to policy.

### Track performance

- Open **Reports** for booking and revenue trends.
- Monitor peak hours and sport demand.
- Use insights to optimize slot pricing and utilization.

## 4) Trainer Guide

### Set up training batches

1. Go to **Trainer Dashboard**.
2. Create a batch with sport, level, schedule, and capacity.
3. Publish batch for player discovery.

### Run sessions

- Mark attendance per session.
- Post updates and announcements.
- Track payment status and pending dues.

## 5) Payments and Receipts

- Payments are processed through Razorpay checkout.
- Verify booking status after payment completion.
- In case of failed payment, retry from booking page.
- For refunds, check booking policy and support workflow.

## 6) Common Issues and Fixes

### Cannot log in

- Verify email/OTP validity and expiry.
- Check spam folder for magic link.
- Retry with the same registered account.

### Booking not confirmed

- Check payment status first.
- Refresh bookings page after 30-60 seconds.
- If debited but not confirmed, contact support with transaction ID.

### Rating not updating

- Confirm match type is `COMPETITIVE` or `TOURNAMENT`.
- Ensure final result was submitted correctly.
- Wait briefly for sync, then refresh profile.

## 7) Best Practices

- Keep profile and city updated for better discovery.
- Join matches close to your true level for meaningful rating movement.
- Record complete scorelines for better performance insights.
- Use official match completion flow to avoid missing stats.

## 8) Need Help

When contacting support, share:

- Registered email or phone
- Role (Player/Venue Owner/Trainer)
- Sport and feature area (Booking, Rating, Training, etc.)
- Screenshot and timestamp of issue
