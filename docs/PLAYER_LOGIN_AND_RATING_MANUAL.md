# Player Manual: Login and Check Sportza Rating

This guide helps players log in to Sportza and view their rating correctly.

## 1) Login to Sportza

Sportza supports multiple login methods:

- Google Sign-In (SSO)
- Email OTP
- Magic Link (email)

### Steps

1. Open the Sportza app or website.
2. Tap or click **Login**.
3. Choose your preferred method:
   - **Google Sign-In**: select your Google account and continue.
   - **Email OTP**: enter email, receive OTP, then verify.
   - **Magic Link**: enter email and open the link sent to your inbox.
4. After successful verification, you are logged in to your player account.

## 2) Go to Your Rating

After login:

1. Open your **Profile**.
2. Go to **Stats**, **Rating**, or **Performance** section (label may vary by screen).
3. Select the sport you want to view (for example: Badminton, Football, Cricket).
4. If available, select format (for example: Singles, Doubles, or Overall).

You should now see:

- Your current rating number
- Confidence tier (for example: unranked, beginner, developing)
- Rated matches count
- Recent rating changes (if history is visible in your build)

## 3) Understand What You Are Seeing

- A new player starts at **1000** rating for each sport/format track.
- Ratings are tracked separately per sport and format.
- Only **COMPETITIVE** and **TOURNAMENT** matches affect rating.
- Friendly/open-play/casual sessions do not change rating.

## 4) Why Rating May Not Move Much

Small changes can happen when:

- You beat a much lower-rated opponent (expected result).
- The match type is not rating-eligible.
- The account has already gained the daily cap (+80 max gain per 24h per sport/format).

Larger changes usually happen when:

- You beat stronger opponents.
- You are a newer player with fewer rated matches.

## 5) If You Cannot See a Rating

Try these checks:

1. Confirm you are logged into the correct account.
2. Play at least one rating-eligible match for that sport/format.
3. Refresh profile and sign in again.
4. Check if the sport is active on the platform for your account.
5. Contact support with:
   - your registered email/phone
   - sport + format
   - approximate match date/time

## 6) Quick FAQ

**Q: Why are Singles and Doubles ratings different?**  
A: Sportza tracks each format separately.

**Q: Why did I lose points after a draw?**  
A: If your team was expected to win, a draw can reduce rating slightly.

**Q: Why is my confidence tier low even with good wins?**  
A: Confidence increases mainly with number of rated matches played.
